More than a rating app

01

Rank, don't rate

No agonizing over stars. Pick "liked it, fine, or didn't," compare a few head-to-head matchups, and 3Wood computes your personal 0–10 score for every course.

02

A feed of real rounds

See the courses your friends play, save, and love — then tap through to their full ranked list. Your crew's taste beats any anonymous review site.

03

Friends' scores, side by side

Open any course and see exactly how everyone in your circle scored it. Settle the "best track in town" debate with receipts.

04

16,000+ US courses

Search by name or city, or explore the map. Jot a want-to-play list so the next trip plans itself.

05

Find your foursome

Match your contacts to find friends already on 3Wood, and invite the rest with a personal link.

06

Climb the leaderboard

Every course you log moves you up. Bragging rights included.
