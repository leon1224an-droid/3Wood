Email 3woodapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a couple of days.

How do course scores work?

You never pick a number. Say whether you liked a course, thought it was fine, or didn't love it, then answer a few head-to-head comparisons against courses you've already played. 3Wood turns those choices into your personal 0–10 score for every course. Scores re-balance automatically as you log more golf.

How do I fix a ranking I disagree with?

Open the course and tap Update my ranking to redo its comparisons, or Remove from my courses to take it off your list entirely.

Why link a phone number?

It's optional. If you link your number, friends who have you in their contacts can find you with Find friends → Find from contacts. Your number is never shown to anyone, and you can unlink it any time from Profile.

How do I report a problem with another user?

Every profile and review has a report option (the ••• menu or a long-press). You can also block a user from their profile — their activity disappears from your feed, leaderboard, and reviews. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, rankings, reviews, and lists. It cannot be undone.

A course is missing or has wrong details

Course data comes from OpenGolfAPI (ODbL). Email us the course name and city and we'll get it fixed in a data refresh.
